I started my tech career working with databases, writing SQL, Stored Procedures, building Data Warehouses and Data Lakes so my company could present meaningful insights to their clients both in the private and public sector. Since then, I have traveled the world leveraging that knowledge, learning, and creating a vast array of solutions in the Business Intelligence space until I successfully moved to Full Stack development and early Machine Learning days during my Master's Degree program.

After getting my Master's Degree with concentration in Databases and Machine Learning,in Boston, I started to explore Full Stack opportunities and, during the last year of my graduate program, I developed a complete desktop Java solution from scratch for my university. The solution aimed to assist and automate internal quality assurance efforts by taking one or multiple test plan spreadsheets that outlined the quality certification process for internal use  software. At the end, a report was produced with the results automating and exposing important metrics for the deparment.

Since that opportunity, I worked in the fintech space building Full Stack B2B solutions and Bluetooth Low Energy software for companies that aimed to modernize real estate buildings in NY, Boston, and San Francisco.

In early 2025, I left my job at the Department of Labor and explored a few opportunities outside my comfort zone in various industries (non-tech). I am very appreciative of having this exposure  as it gave me an incredible amount of perspective and I was able to observe up close how technology can affect blue collar jobs in positive and negative ways.

After a few months of exploration, I decided to focus now on the AI industry and I have been working on various AI projects, including building Neural Network applications, developing AI-driven solutions, and exploring the latest advancements in artificial intelligence. I am now working on acquiring skills and certifications in Deep Learning and Neural Networks where I hope to apply my knowledge in AI and LLMs to create innovative solutions that can make a significant impact in various fields.

I have always been passionate about technology and how it can be used to solve real-world problems. My journey in tech has been driven by a desire to understand the intricacies of software development and the impact it can have on businesses and society. I believe that technology should not only be functional but also intuitive and user-friendly, which is why I strive to create solutions that have a positive user experience and impact.
